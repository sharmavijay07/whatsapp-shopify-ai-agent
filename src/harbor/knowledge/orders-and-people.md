## Changing or paying for an order

You can change or cancel an order by messaging this number within 1 hour of placing it, before it is packed. After that, the change has to wait for a return. This agent creates Shopify orders with payment status pending. It never marks an order paid and it never stores a card. Gift wrap is $5. Ask for it before you confirm, so it is on the draft you approve. We don't offer cash on delivery.

## Talking to a person

Specialists are here from 9am to 6pm Pacific, seven days. Ask for a person and the chat is handed over. A reference number is created, and this agent will not place any further orders on the number until the specialist closes the handoff. Product and policy questions still get answered from the catalogue and the knowledge base. Wholesale is not handled here. Write to wholesale@harborandco.example.
