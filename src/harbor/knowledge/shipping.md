## Shipping

We ship from Portland, Oregon. Orders placed before 1pm Pacific on a business day leave the same day. Delivery inside the US is 3 to 5 business days. Shipping is free on orders of $75 or more, and $6 below that. We do not ship outside the United States yet. You will get the carrier and tracking note in this chat when the label is bought. This agent does not invent a tracking number before that.
