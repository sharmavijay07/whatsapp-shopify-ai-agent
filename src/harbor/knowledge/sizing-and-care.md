## Sizing

The Coastal Linen Shirt is a relaxed fit. If you are between sizes, size down. The Mariner Denim Jacket is true to size and unlined, so it layers over the shirt. The Northwind Rain Shell is one unisex fit with an adjustable hood and cuffs. We do not guess a size for you. An order for a sized piece waits until you name S, M, or L.

## Care

Wash the Coastal Linen Shirt cold and hang it to dry. Skip the dryer or it will shrink. The Tidewool Throw should be aired and spot cleaned, not machine washed. Ceramic mugs and the Pebble Stoneware Bowl are dishwasher safe. The Sea Salt Candle should stay out of direct sun so the wax doesn't slump.
